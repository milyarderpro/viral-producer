## P-000004 — Food and Household Knowledge

Did you know?

Pineapple is one fruit made by fusing fruits from 100 to 200 flowers

Black, white, and green peppercorns come from one plant but are harvested and processed differently

Cloves are unopened flower buds picked before blooming, then dried into the familiar spice

Popcorn pops because water trapped inside each hard kernel turns to steam and builds pressure

Around 40 gallons of maple sap may be needed to make one gallon of syrup

Vanilla flavor comes from orchid fruits whose familiar aroma appears only after drying and curing
