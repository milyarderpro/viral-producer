## P-000006 — Animals and Nature

Did you know?

Greater short-horned lizards can squirt blood from their eyes up to three feet

Binturongs smell like buttered popcorn because their urine contains the same aroma compound

Armored searobins use thickened fin rays to walk across the deep seafloor

Sea cucumbers breathe by pumping water through two respiratory trees beside their digestive tract

Kākāpō are the only known parrot species that use lek breeding to attract mates

Wood frogs can spend up to eight months frozen, with no heartbeat or breathing

## P-000009 — Geography and History

Did you know?

The United States’ easternmost and westernmost points around the 180° meridian are 63 miles apart in Alaska

London Bridge was dismantled in England and reconstructed in Lake Havasu City, Arizona

The Great Lakes hold about 21% of the world’s supply of surface fresh water

Reaching Point Roberts, Washington, by land from the rest of the state requires driving through Canada

Texas’s highest point, Guadalupe Peak, is part of a 260-to-270-million-year-old fossil reef complex

Mount Whitney, the lower 48’s tallest mountain, sits within 85 miles of North America’s lowest place
