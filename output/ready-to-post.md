## P-000006 — Animals and Nature

Did you know?

Greater short-horned lizards can squirt blood from their eyes up to three feet

Binturongs smell like buttered popcorn because their urine contains the same aroma compound

Armored searobins use thickened fin rays to walk across the deep seafloor

Sea cucumbers breathe by pumping water through two respiratory trees beside their digestive tract

Kākāpō are the only known parrot species that use lek breeding to attract mates

Wood frogs can spend up to eight months frozen, with no heartbeat or breathing
